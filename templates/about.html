Plan Your Visit

Everything you need to know before visiting Theater Dramus.

Location

You can find us at 12 Gaslight Row, right next to the historic clock tower. We're just a five-minute walk from the central station.

Opening Times

Our doors open 45 minutes before the performance begins. If you arrive late, our staff will help you find a seat at an appropriate break in the show.

Accessibility

Theater Dramus offers step-free access, wheelchair spaces in every row, and hearing loop systems at all performances. Please contact the box office in advance if you require additional assistance.
